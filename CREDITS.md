## Open-source and language-resource acknowledgements

Logos builds on the work of many open-source projects and language-resource communities, including:

- **sherpa-onnx** by k2-fsa, providing the local speech-synthesis runtime
- **ONNX Runtime** by Microsoft, providing model inference support
- **Node.js**, providing the bundled JavaScript runtime used by Logos workers
- **Kokoro-family text-to-speech models**, providing English and multilingual voices
- **VCTK / VITS-VCTK-derived resources**, providing additional English speaker voices
- **Princeton WordNet**, providing English lexical information
- **CC-CEDICT**, providing Chinese-English dictionary data
- **Tatoeba**, providing example-sentence data
- **Mozilla PDF.js / pdfjs-dist**, providing PDF text-processing support
- **@breezystack/lamejs**, providing MP3 encoding functionality

Logos would not be possible without the work of these projects, their maintainers, dataset creators, contributors, speakers, and communities.
